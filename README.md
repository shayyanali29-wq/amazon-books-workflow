# Amazon Books Workflow

**Amazon Books Workflow is an author-led, step-by-step system for taking a book from an early idea or existing draft through research, planning, writing, editing, publication preparation, and launch.** It brings the creative work and the publishing work into one organized process, while adapting each stage to the book’s readers, genre, format, marketplace, and the author’s goals.

The workflow is designed to make decisions visible and revisable. It distinguishes observed evidence from interpretation, keeps the author in control, and does not promise sales, rankings, or publication outcomes.

## What the workflow does

1. **Establishes the project.** It identifies the book and author, reviews any existing notes or manuscript, and determines what has already been decided. For a new book, it gathers the essential creative and publishing details with focused questions, one at a time. It does not make an existing author repeat an intake process unnecessarily.
2. **Researches readers and the market when useful.** When the author needs help choosing or testing a concept, it examines relevant marketplace evidence and reader signals for the selected Amazon marketplace. It records what was reviewed, when it was reviewed, what the evidence may support, and what remains uncertain.
3. **Helps choose and position the book.** It compares reader fit, author fit, distinctiveness, and the effort required to produce each viable concept. It can recommend a direction and explain the evidence behind that recommendation; the author makes the choice.
4. **Builds an agreed plan.** It turns the selected direction into a clear reader promise, scope, structure, and chapter or section outline. It develops a representative sample and uses the author’s feedback to refine the voice and plan before substantial drafting.
5. **Writes in manageable stages.** It drafts the manuscript step by step, generally one chapter or section at a time. After each stage, it checks the work against the agreed purpose, voice, reader value, and continuity, records open questions, and incorporates the author’s revisions before moving forward.
6. **Edits and checks the complete manuscript.** It reviews structure, chapter function, continuity, factual support, clarity, voice, copy, and proofing as distinct concerns. For nonfiction, it tracks claims and sources; for fiction, it tracks story details and unresolved threads. It does not invent citations, credentials, personal experience, endorsements, or results.
7. **Prepares files and publishing information.** It uses current official platform guidance for the chosen marketplace and format, and helps check the manuscript, cover, layout, front and back matter, metadata, and rights. Ebook and print requirements are treated as separate production needs.
8. **Plans a truthful launch and learns from results.** It helps prepare reader-facing descriptions and an author-appropriate marketing plan. If the author chooses advertising, the workflow defines a test and spending limits before any campaign begins. Actual sales, royalties, costs, and ad results are reported separately from estimates.

## A careful, author-controlled process

The author approves the book brief, outline, creative direction, and revisions. Research informs decisions; it does not replace judgment or guarantee demand. Project notes can preserve sources, decisions, chapter status, and production checks so work can continue coherently across sessions.

The skill can prepare publishing materials and checklists, but the author remains in control of their publishing account. It does not request passwords, banking details, tax identifiers, or identity documents, and it does not upload a book, change account settings, or spend advertising money without explicit authorization. Specialized medical, legal, financial, and safety claims may need review by a qualified professional.

## Who it is for

Use the workflow for a new Amazon-focused book, a concept that needs research, a manuscript in progress, or a finished draft approaching publication. It supports fiction, nonfiction, children’s books, and visual or activity-based formats. For an existing manuscript-only request, it preserves the established direction and focuses on the writing or editing work requested.

## Use it in Codex

Install this repository as a skill folder named `amazon-books-workflow` in your Codex skills directory. Then invoke:

```text
$amazon-books-workflow
```

Start with a new book, or point the skill to the existing project files you want it to continue from. The workflow will identify the next useful decision and proceed in agreed stages.
